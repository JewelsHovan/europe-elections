Interactive map

Each of the 96 metropolitan departments and 5 overseas departments is coloured by the layer you choose. Tap or click a department for its full record. The projected runoff layer is a model, not a poll: it takes each department's lean from its 2022 and 2024 results and shifts it to the national share you set with the slider.

Actual 2022 runoff: Le Pen 41.5% nationally against Macron. She led in 28 departments: all 5 overseas, plus 23 in the northeast, the Mediterranean rim, Corsica and the Garonne valley. Which bloc led the first round of the June 2024 legislative election. The RN and its Ciotti allies led in 67 of 101 departments; the left led in the big cities, Paris and its inner suburbs, and the Southwest. Change in the far-right first-round vote: 2022 presidential (Le Pen + Zemmour + Dupont-Aignan) to 2024 legislative (RN + allies + Reconquête). The largest gains were in the rural Southwest. Corsica's drop reflects local candidates in 2024. The RN list led by Jordan Bardella won 31.4% nationally in June 2024 and topped the poll in 92 of 96 metropolitan departments (all but Paris, Hauts-de-Seine, Seine-Saint-Denis and Val-de-Marne).
